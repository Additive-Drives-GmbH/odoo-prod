manaTec Purchase Employee

Extends Purchase with needed fields and views

This module extends the Odoo Purchase and HR modules to provide better employee tracking and shorthand identification on purchase orders.

Features

  • HR Employee Extensions: Adds a shorthand field to the employee model and public employee profile for easy identification.
  • Purchase Order Extensions: Adds an employee_id and a computed shorthand field to Purchase Orders (positioned after the source document field).
  • Automatic Synchronization: Automatically populates the shorthand when an employee is selected, and propagates updates from employee records to linked purchase orders.
  • Advanced Search & Filtering: Provides dedicated search fields and quick filters for "Shorthand" in both Purchase Order and Request for Quotation search views.

Technical Details

  • Models Modified: hr.employee, hr.employee.public, and purchase.order.
  • Views Modified: Form views for employees and purchase orders, tree/list views, and search views for purchase orders and requests for quotation.
  • Dependencies: base, purchase, hr
