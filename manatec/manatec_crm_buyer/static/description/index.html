manaTec CRM Buyer

Buyer Contact for Leads and Opportunities

This module extends Odoo CRM Leads/Opportunities with a Buyer field, linking the customer-side purchasing contact (res.partner) and storing their email for quick reference, shown in the Contacts tab under Contact Information.

Features

  • Lead/Opportunity Extension: Adds buyer_id (Many2one to res.partner) and buyer_email (Char) fields to crm.lead.
  • Contact Information: Shows the Buyer and Buyer Email fields in the "Contacts" tab, under "Contact Information", next to the existing contact fields.
  • Computed & Read-only: The Buyer Email field is computed from the selected Buyer contact's email and cannot be edited manually.

Technical Details

  • Models Modified: crm.lead: Adds the buyer_id (Many2one) field and the buyer_email (Char, computed and stored, read-only) field.
  • Views Modified: crm.crm_lead_view_form: Adds the Buyer and Buyer Email fields inside the "Contact Information" group of the "Contacts" tab.
  • Dependencies: crm
